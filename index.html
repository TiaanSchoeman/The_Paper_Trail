Our Story

Twenty-seven years on Rosemary Lane

The Paper Trail opened in spring 1998 in a former printer's shop at the top of Rosemary Lane. The presses were long gone, but the smell of ink lingered in the walls — and, we like to think, still does.

We've always stocked what we love: literary fiction, poetry that risks something, non-fiction that reads like a novel. And three evenings a week, we push the shelves back and host something else — a reading, a debate, a screening, a dinner.

The shop is still ours. Come in.
